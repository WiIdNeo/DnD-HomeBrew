`Hoddafjall` is an old dwarvish kingdom. 

It is located in the [Djúpagull](../Places/Edelberge.md). While the entrances are mainly in south to middle of the mountains the kingdom is called to expand further. But the dwarfs do not make any call on this as they do not want others to know.

# History

The kingdom founded after the [dwarfs](../../../../../Lore/Races/Dwarfs/Dwarfs.md) killed the dragon having it's hoard there. Then they started to build their kingdom from this hoard. 

In [gold wars](Gold_Wars.md) the dwarfs fought the dragons and claimed the mountains as their lands.
