The `Edelberge` are a large mountain chain on `Continent A`. 

They got their name from large gem and gold depots. `Edelberge` (loosely translated: Gem Mountains) is the name of the humans of [windmark](../Windmark/Windmark.md).

The Mountains feature different folks and creatures. Deep inside the mountains there lays the great halls of [Hoddafjall](../Hoddafjall/Hoddafjall.md) (Hoard mountain). They call the mountains `Djúpagull` (Deep Gold).

The name of the dwarven kingdom alludes to the old [dragon](../../../../../Lore/Dragons/Dragon.md) cave layed in the first cave of it. 
There are still some dragons in the high north of the `Edelberge`, but the number or their hoard is not known.
