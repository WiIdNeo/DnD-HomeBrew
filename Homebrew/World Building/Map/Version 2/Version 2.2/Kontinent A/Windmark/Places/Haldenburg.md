# Haldenburg

Haldenburg is one of the most southern places of the [Windmark](../Windmark.md). It is located at the coast of `Ocean 1`.

The big river flowing through the city is called ` Goldflow`. It is used to trade to the dwarven kingdom `Kingdom 1` inside `Lucky Mountains`. They recive a lot of gold and gems what makes the city wealthy and popular. In exchange they trade peat, what is processed in the swamps around the city.

# Destricts

The city is dominated by the large river. At first it was only located on the north side. So you find the main city on this side. That main part of the city is dominated by two hills. 

One is called the `Gnadenberg` (Mercy Mountain) and features the main tempels of the city. The other one is the `Haldenburg`, a castle partial into the mountain. 

Between those you find the main city where the citizens live. At the east you find the Destrict of craftsman.

On the south side of ` Goldflow` you find the slums. It's rarely higher located than water, so it is easily overflown. To avoid the huts been ripped away they are placed on poles. But still those huts are cold, windy and humid, so people are sick an feable. Also there are no wells, and the near water is all salty, because of tides even the river is salty nearly along all the city. So to get fresh water the people need to carry it a long way.
